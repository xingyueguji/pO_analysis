## 1. What was asked

The user asked for a MET-based fit to compare with the lepton-pT fit, with:

- **the same events**: "a counterpart from lepton_pT with m_T > 40 GeV, but the
  discriminant switched to MET";
- **the same multijet treatment**: in-fit ABCD, per rapidity group, for muon and
  electron, and the pT-dependent transfer factor (fake factor) for the electron;
- **a MET-modelling systematic** (MET resolution from the Z recoil); user
  decision: in the MET fit **only**, not in the leppt_mt40 inputs.

The goal is to see whether the electron W⁻ forward/backward drop survives in a
MET-shape fit.

## 2. The model

met_mt40 = PF MET (60 × 2 GeV, 0–120) fitted on exactly the leppt_mt40 events
(pT > 25 && m_T > 40). Everything the leppt_mt40 fit has, met_mt40 has:

| piece | leppt_mt40 | met_mt40 |
|---|---|---|
| SR templates | lepton pT 24–100 | PF MET 0–120 |
| in-fit ABCD regions (m_T plane) | B (iso, m_T<30), C40 (anti-iso, m_T>40), D (anti-iso, m_T<30) | **identical** (same counts, same CR dirs) |
| rapidity groups (abcdy) | fwd y10–11 / bwd y0–1 / cen y2–9 | **identical** |
| muon SR multijet | anti-iso m_T>40 pT shape, total A0 | anti-iso m_T>40 **MET** shape, total A0 |
| electron SR multijet | T0 × f(pT) × C40(pT) | T0 × Σ_pT f(pT) C40(pT, MET), the same f |
| electron slope nuisance | qcdFF_ele_{Wp,Wm} | same names, MET-projected twins |
| residual lnN κ | μ 1.09 / e 1.05 | same |
| gen fiducial (σ = r × σ_gen) | m_T > 40 set | **same** → σ, A_ch, R_FB compare directly |
| shape systematics | nPDF qcdScale alphaS muSF/eleSF | same **+ metResMu / metResEle** |

The fake-factor model on MET evaluates every anti-iso event with pT ≥ 100 at
105 GeV, the convention its normalization ⟨g⟩_D already uses. A first version
used bin centres up to 205 GeV: f then reached ~50 for the 0.8% of C40 above
100 GeV and inflated the low-MET bins ×2–3, because m_T > 40 with MET < 10
requires pT > 40.

### MET resolution (`correction/recoil_res.C`, `skim/met_res.h`)

Z recoil, u_⊥ RMS over ±200 GeV, data vs embedded DY MC (log
`correction/logs/recoil_res.log`):

| | data | MC | k = data/MC | σ_add = σ_MC √(k²−1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z→μμ (364 pairs) | 8.27 ± 0.31 GeV | 7.50 GeV | 1.103 ± 0.041 (2.5σ) | 3.49 GeV |
| Z→ee (225 pairs) | 9.77 ± 0.46 GeV | 7.73 GeV | 1.265 ± 0.060 (4.4σ) | 5.98 GeV |

The u_∥ mean difference (the response) is printed, not varied: +1.87 ± 1.12 GeV
(μ), +0.86 ± 1.69 GeV (e).

- **+1σ:** each MC event's MET vector is smeared by Gauss(σ_add) per component.
  The smearing is deterministic (seeded by the entry number), and m_T is
  recomputed, so events can migrate across m_T > 40.
- **−1σ:** the mirror, 2·nominal − Up, floored at 0.
- **Treatment:** one nuisance per flavour (independent Z measurements), group
  `metres`, not area-normalized.

m_T > 40 acceptance shifts at +1σ (skim logs):

| sample | μ | e |
|---|---|---|
| W⁺ | −0.34% | −0.87% |
| W⁻ | −0.25% | −0.76% |
| DY | +0.71% | +17.7% (small absolute) |
| ttbar | −0.04% | −0.23% |

On the met_mt40 signal templates: −0.2…−0.4% (μ) and −0.6…−1.1% (e) per lab
bin in normalization, plus the shape.
