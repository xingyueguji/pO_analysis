# Iteration 2026-10-13 — W gen fiducial with m_T > 40 for leppt_mt40

**Status:** gen cross sections and the observables chain regenerated locally on
2026-10-13. **No refit needed** for σ, A_ch or R_FB: the fitted r's do not depend
on σ_gen. Only the profiled (σ_W, σ_Z) contour has to be re-run on lxplus,
because it bakes σ_gen into its σ-POI map. The fit itself is the 2026-10-05 one
(`2026-10-01_ele-ppEGM-recoSF-again.md`); every input of it is unchanged.

## 1. What changed (user decision, 2026-10-13)

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| W gen lepton source | `HiGenParticleAna/hi` (filtered pT > 5, \|η\| < 2.5, no W stored): highest-pT lepton of flavour + charge | EventTree `mc*` block: the status-1 lepton whose mother is the W (or, for an FSR copy, whose grandmother is) |
| W neutrino | not used | the `mc*` neutrino with mother W (exactly one per event) |
| fiducial, `met` fit | bare ℓ pT > 25, \|η_lab\| < 2.4 | unchanged |
| fiducial, `leppt_mt40` fit | the same (no m_T cut; the m_T > 40 efficiency was taken from MC) | **+ m_T(ℓ, ν) > 40 GeV**, the gen twin of the reco cut |
| Z fiducial | `hi`, lead/sub 15/10, \|η\| < 2.4, 60–120 | unchanged |

Why `mc*`: `hi` drops the neutrino when it is forward or soft, in **22% (W⁺) /
9.3% (W⁻)** of the events with a fiducial m_T > 40 lepton (scratch study, 400k
events per sample). m_T would have been wrong with no error message.

Code: `skim/gen_xsec.C` (+ wrapper `skim/run_gen_xsec.sh`, log
`skim/logs/gen_xsec.log`), `skim/skim_common.h` (`kPtDiscMtCut = 40`, now also
read by `skim.C`), `plotting/disc_variants.h` (`GenFid`: disc → set),
`analysis/fiducial_yields.C` (required `disc` argument),
`plotting/xsec_{fiducial,contour,summary}.C`, `analysis/run_observables.sh`.
Fork: `test/run_pO_fits.sh` (picks `gen_xsec_fid_mt40.txt` for
`--disc leppt_mt40`), `test/sync_lxplus.sh` (uploads both sidecars),
comments in `test/my_script/make_pO_simfit_cards.sh`.

## 2. Gen fiducial cross sections (per lepton flavour, nb)

| | old (`hi`) | new, no m_T | new, m_T > 40 | m_T > 40 / no m_T |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W⁺ | 49.80 | 49.82 | **47.75** | 0.9586 |
| W⁻ | 39.63 | 39.64 | **38.17** | 0.9631 |
| W | 89.43 | 89.46 | **85.93** | 0.9605 |
| Z | 8.769 | 8.769 | 8.769 | — |

- Old vs new without m_T: +0.03%, ≤ 0.08% per bin (MC stat 0.3%/bin). Cause,
  checked event by event: `hi` stores pT on a ~1/64 GeV grid near 25 GeV
  (relative ±5×10⁻⁴, unbiased). 25.0 is a grid point, so a lepton with true
  pT ∈ (25, 25.008) is stored as 25.000 and fails `pT > 25` (107 vs 6 events
  per 297k).
- m_T > 40 acceptance per lab bin y0…y11: W⁺ 0.973 → 0.952 (minimum at y8),
  W⁻ 0.961–0.967.
- EPPS21 nPDF band (68% CL) on the m_T > 40 prediction: W⁺ +2.62/−3.81%,
  W⁻ +2.47/−3.66%, W +2.54/−3.72% (essentially as before).
- Lepton definition: bare and Born (≈ W − ν) leptons keep the same m_T
  acceptance (0.958 both), so the cut adds no μ/e difference. Born/bare
  σ_fid = 1.022 (μ) / 1.045 (e), unchanged.
