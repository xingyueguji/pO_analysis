## 2. How ttbar enters

- **Skim** (`skim/skim_common.h` `kTT`, `run_all.sh` sample `TT`): the
  standard selection, reco-charge regions (ttbar fills W⁺ and W⁻ alike), the
  muon/electron SFs and the LHE member twins like every MC file. One file for
  every channel → `*_TT_hist.root` / `*_TT_MC_hist.root`.
- **ABCD QCD** (`correction/qcd_abcd.C`): subtracted with the other EWK MC in
  every region; in B it is split out of `zB` into its own `ttB` (the CRB `z`
  rides r_Z, ttbar must not); `ttC40`/`ttD` are the ttbar parts of the frozen
  CRC/CRD `ewk`.
- **Per-y QCD split** (`plotting/mtandmet.C` `qcdWeights`): ttbar is in the
  EWK subtracted from the low-MET data excess.
- **Combine inputs**: a `ttbar` template in every W region of both
  discriminants, in both Z peaks and in `{Wp,Wm}_CRB`; its LHE and lepton-SF
  Up/Down templates; the sidecars list `ttbar` for every shape systematic.
- **Fit model** (fork `make_pO_simfit_cards.sh`): process `ttbar`, id 20,
  in every W channel, both Z peaks and CRB; **frozen at absolute MC** (no map
  matches it); `lumi` 1.03 and its own **`ttbar_xsec` lnN, κ = 1.15**
  (`TTBAR_LNN`; = scale ±13% ⊕ EPPS21 ~3%, 68% CL); plus the shape rows its
  sidecars list (nPDF, qcdScale, alphaS — area-normalized, shape only — and
  muSF / eleSF). Inside CRC/CRD's `ewk` it carries lumi only.
- **Display**: stacked LAST in every stack (colour kOrange+3, the 8th of the
  palette) so every other entry keeps its colour.

## 3. Decisions taken by default (for the user to confirm before the fit)

1. κ(`ttbar_xsec`) = **1.15**.
2. The LHE shape nuisances act on ttbar with the SAME θ as on the W/DY
   templates (one θ per family for the whole card — the existing convention).
3. ttbar inside the frozen CRC/CRD `ewk`: lumi only, no `ttbar_xsec`.

## 7. Fit (leppt_mt40, abcd; downloaded 2026-10-16)

**Fit quality.** All six fits (grand / μ / e × lab / fb): status 0, covQual 3,
Asimov closure PASS (now including ttbar_xsec θ = 0), the `--statonly`
companion vs the conditioned covariance ≤ 0.06%. All 24 contour scans have
the full 2501 points and one contour piece.

**The gen fiducial σ is the m_T > 40 set everywhere** (the point of the
2026-10-13 change):
- the fitted σ-POI maps: σ_W = 85.928216 nb (lab) / 73.575222 (fb) = the sums
  of `gen_xsec_fid_mt40.txt` (the no-m_T set would be 89.45 / 76.70), and all

  ≤ 0.002 nb on σ_Z.

**Verdict:** ttbar enters as a ~0.5% central, charge-symmetric background and

+≤0.002, e/μ and R_FB unchanged), with no new tension.
