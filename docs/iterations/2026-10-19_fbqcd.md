## 1. What changed

**The fb-binned multijet templates now carry the FB window's share of the
inclusive multijet instead of all of it** (user decision, 2026-10-19).

- The SR multijet template (`qcd`, the fit's `qcd_abcd`, the electron
  fake-factor twins `qcd_abcd_qcdFF_ele_*`) is built rapidity-inclusively per
  (flavour, charge) and split over the rapidity bins by `plotting/mtandmet.C
  qcdWeights`, with weights proportional to the iso-pass MET < 30 data − EWK
  excess of each bin.
- Lab bins tile |y| < 2.4, the range of the inclusive template; their weights
  sum to 1 (unchanged).
- The FB bins tile only [−1.7068, 2.4]. Until now their weights were also
  normalized to 1, which put the multijet of y < −1.7068 into the window. Now
  every weight is divided by the LAB sum of the excess, so the FB templates sum
  to the window's share f = Σ_FB / Σ_lab of the inclusive total.

| | f (W⁺) | f (W⁻) | old fb overstatement |
|---|---|---|---|
| μ | 0.9096 | 0.9056 | ×1.10 |
| e | 0.7779 | 0.7448 | ×1.29 / ×1.34 |

Found by `correction/qcd_ysplit.C` (step 1 of the charge-split follow-up, same
day). The share from the per-bin ABCD agrees within 4% (μ 0.874/0.906, e
0.809/0.763). Alternative not taken: window-restricted control regions from
the new `_FB` skim histograms (needs fork changes).

Also new, without effect on the fit inputs: the W skim stores rapidity-binned
copies of the (iso, m_T) ABCD plane, `h_iso_mt_{mu,ele}{Plus,Minus}_y<i>[_FB]`
(re-skim of Wmu + Wel, every old histogram bit-identical,
