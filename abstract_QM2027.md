# QM 2027 abstract — W/Z in pO (draft 2026-09-08)

## Title

Measurement of W and Z boson production in proton-oxygen collisions at √s_NN = 9.62 TeV with CMS

## Abstract (full, ~360 words / ~2200 characters)

Electroweak bosons are clean probes of the nuclear initial state: produced in hard parton scatterings at the earliest stage of the collision, they decay to leptons that do not interact strongly with the surrounding medium and are reconstructed with high purity. The CMS measurements of W and Z boson production in pPb collisions at √s_NN = 5.02 and 8.16 TeV revealed significant nuclear modifications of the parton densities of the lead nucleus, and these data now anchor the global nuclear PDF fits at high Q². The 2025 proton-oxygen run of the LHC extends this program to a light nucleus, where the mass-number dependence of the nuclear modifications is essentially unconstrained by high-Q² data and the global fits rely on extrapolation. Oxygen is moreover an isoscalar nucleus, so the W charge asymmetry is free of the neutron-excess isospin effects that dominate it in pPb collisions and probes more directly the flavor dependence of the nuclear modifications.

We present the first measurement of W and Z boson production in pO collisions at √s_NN = 9.62 TeV, using data corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 46.5 nb⁻¹ recorded by CMS. W bosons are reconstructed in the μν and eν channels, requiring an isolated lepton with pT > 25 GeV and |η_lab| < 2.4, and Z bosons in the μμ and ee channels in the mass window 60 < m_ℓℓ < 120 GeV. The multijet background is determined from data. The fiducial W⁺, W⁻ and Z cross sections are extracted from a simultaneous fit of the muon and electron channels, and the W cross sections are measured differentially in the lepton pseudorapidity. The lepton charge asymmetry and the forward-backward ratios between the proton-going and oxygen-going directions in the nucleon-nucleon center-of-mass frame probe the oxygen parton densities for 10⁻³ ≲ x ≲ 10⁻¹, spanning the shadowing and antishadowing regions, with many experimental and theoretical uncertainties canceling in the ratios. The results are compared with next-to-leading-order predictions with free-proton PDFs and with the EPPS21, nCTEQ15HQ, nNNPDF3.0 and TUJU21 nuclear PDF sets. They provide the first constraints on the partonic structure of a light nucleus at LHC energies and the initial-state reference for hard-probe measurements in OO collisions.

- Fiducial W: lepton pT > 25 GeV, |η_lab| < 2.4 (`skim.C`, `gen_xsec.C::kFidPtMin`). Z: 60 < m_ℓℓ < 120 GeV, leading/subleading lepton pT > 15/10 GeV (`skim_Zmm`/`skim_Zee`).
- CM frame: y_CM = y_lab − 0.3466 (= ½ ln 2), proton-going = forward (`analysis/analysis_helpers.h`). Lab |η| < 2.4 ⇒ η_CM ∈ [−2.75, 2.05]; F/B pairs cover |η_CM| < 2.05 (`kYEdgesFB`).
- x range: x_O ≈ (m_W/√s_NN)·exp(∓y_CM) with m_W/√s_NN = 8.4×10⁻³ ⇒ ≈1×10⁻³ (forward) to ≈0.13 (backward).
- nPDF sets: EPPS21, nCTEQ15HQ, nNNPDF3.0, TUJU21 = the MCFM R_pO curves in `plotting/pQCDLightIon/` (`plotRpOtheory.C`); MC = POWHEG CT18ANLO × EPPS21 (O16).
- pPb context (from memory, verify before citing): W→μν at 5.02 TeV, 34.6 nb⁻¹ (PLB 750 (2015) 565); W→μν at 8.16 TeV, 173.4 nb⁻¹, first observation of nuclear modifications (PLB 800 (2020) 135048); Drell-Yan/Z at 8.16 TeV (JHEP 05 (2021) 182).
- "First measurement": confirm no other LHC experiment shows W/Z in pO before QM27; otherwise "first CMS measurement".
- "Z cross section": the fit currently returns r_Z; drop the Z σ from the abstract if only the W σ's will be quoted.
